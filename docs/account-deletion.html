Delete Your EMPlay Account

You can request deletion of your EMPlay account and associated data at any time.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email and display name)
  • Your locally saved movies and TV shows (stored on your device)

How to request deletion

  1. Open a new issue on our GitHub repository
  2. Title the issue: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address associated with your account

We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 days.

Local data

Saved movies and TV shows are stored only on your device. You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the app.

EMPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

EMPlay does not collect or share personal data with third parties.

Account & Authentication

If you create an account, your email and display name are stored via Firebase Authentication. This data is used solely to identify your session and is not shared.

Local Storage

Saved movies and TV shows are stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Movie and TV show data is fetched from the following third-party APIs:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB)
  • Movie of the Night (MOTN)

Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details on their data practices.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
